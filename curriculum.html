Curriculum

Our curriculum is designed by students, for students. Each module focuses on one real-world money skill and ends with steps teens can take right away.

Modules

MODULE 01

Budgeting Basics

Track where money comes from and where it goes, and build a simple budget that gives every dollar a job.

MODULE 02

Saving and Paying Yourself First

Why saving comes before spending, how to automate it, and how to set goals you will actually reach.

MODULE 03

Building Credit as a Teen

How credit scores work and how to start a credit history the smart way, from authorized user to secured card.

MODULE 04

Debt and Interest

The difference between good and bad debt, how interest adds up, and strategies to pay debt down fast.

MODULE 05

Investing and the Stock Market

What it means to own a share of a business, how investments grow over time, and how dividends work.

MODULE 06

Managing Risk

Why spreading your bets matters, the basics of diversification, and how to avoid common money traps.

Our Approach

We keep every lesson practical: plain language, real examples, and no jargon without an explanation. Because our workshops are taught by teens, students feel comfortable asking the questions they actually have. When we can, we bring in finance professionals so students hear how these ideas work in real careers.
