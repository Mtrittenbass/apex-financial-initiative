Workshops

Our workshops are free, interactive sessions for teens, taught by students like us. We cover the money basics school often skips, including budgeting, saving, credit, debt, and investing, and we bring them to schools and community organizations. You don’t need a big group. We’re just as happy to work with a handful of students as a full classroom.

How It Works

01

Request a workshop

Tell us about your group using the form below: who they are, how many students, and what you’d like covered. Any group size works, from a handful of students to a full class.

02

We tailor the session

We pick topics from our curriculum that fit your students’ age and interests, and can invite a finance professional to join.

03

Students learn by doing

Our team leads an interactive session with real examples and clear next steps students can act on right away.
