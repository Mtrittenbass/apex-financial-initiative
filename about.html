About Us

We’re a student-led nonprofit based in Bellevue, Washington. We believe financial literacy is a life skill every student deserves to learn, so we teach it ourselves through free workshops for teens, partnerships with community organizations, and our work with the Bellevue Youth City Council.

Meet Our Team

Co-Founder

Marcus Trittenbass

I co-founded Apex Financial Initiative to give teens the practical money skills most schools leave out. I help lead our workshops and build the lessons students see in them. As a member of the Bellevue Youth City Council, I’m working with Akshat to create an action team focused on financial literacy.
