Free, student-led financial literacy workshops for teens in Bellevue, Washington.

Request a Workshop About Us

Our Mission

Most students finish high school without ever learning how money actually works. We’re here to change that. We’re a student-led nonprofit, and we teach teens the essentials of budgeting, saving, credit, debt, and investing through free, hands-on workshops that we lead ourselves.

Beyond the Classroom

01

Finance Professionals in the Room

We invite people who work in finance to join our workshops and share how money decisions play out in real careers and real life. Students get to ask questions directly and see where these skills can take them.

02

Bellevue Youth City Council Action Team

Two of us, co-founders Marcus Trittenbass and Akshat Porwal, serve on the Bellevue Youth City Council. Together we’re creating an action team focused on financial literacy to give more young people in Bellevue access to practical money education.
